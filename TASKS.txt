  RESULTS: Next Race / View Card Summary

Parlay notes:
  - Click 1 horse = win bet; click 2–3 in finish order = parlay (order matters)
  - Parlay tickets get a larger claim (1.25x / 1.5x over exact-order probability)
    so they pay more per dollar than a win bet when they hit
  - Parlay money on a horse as 1st leg moves that horse's live win multiplier
  - Entire race pool is paid to hitting tickets (or returned if none hit)
